## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end architectural luxury tailored to the prime real estate market of Santo Antônio de Jesus and the Bahian interior. The visual direction balances editorial sophistication with cutting-edge spatial technology (360° virtual tours and WebXR immersion).

The identity fuses **Contemporary Luxury** with **Subtle Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian tones, satin champagne accents, and luminous off-white surfaces evoke permanence, architectural prestige, and discreet wealth.
- **Visual Weight:** Generous negative space, whisper-thin borders with satin gradients, and ambient depth that recedes behind content.
- **Motion & Texture:** Ultra-smooth transitions (cubic-bezier easing), soft backdrop blurs on hovering navigation and spatial controllers, and micro-interactions that mirror fine jewelry and precision horology.
- **Target Audience:** High-net-worth buyers, regional investors, diaspora returnees, and luxury lifestyle seekers expecting white-glove digital concierge experiences.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark mode to provide a cinematic, gallery-grade foundation that honors architectural photography and 360° renders.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Champagne Warm Gold):** Reserved for signature actions, tour triggers, price highlights, and key brand accents. Subtle linear gradients shift between `#C29B27` and `#E5C365` for elevated sheen.
- **Secondary (`#1A1C20` / Charcoal Onyx):** Container surfaces, floating sheets, card bodies, and layered control consoles.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` / Spatial Violet):** Reserved exclusively for immersion tags, WebXR, and virtual reality modes, visually separating interactive 3D media from static media.
- **Neutral (`#121316` / Obsidian Deep):** The foundation viewport canvas, maximizing contrast with gold typography and ambient photography.

### Functional Accents
- **Pearl Off-White (`#F8F9FA` to `#F1F3F5`):** High-contrast headline text, primary iconography, and clean light-state accents.
- **Emerald Concierge (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to real-time WhatsApp broker touchpoints, grounded within dark chrome circular holders.
- **Surface Borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Hairline structural dividers that maintain definition without visual noise.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a deliberate contrast between editorial grace and technical precision:
- **Display & Headline Roles (Playfair Display):** Conveys traditional prestige, timeless architecture, and high editorial value. Italics within headlines are encouraged for evocative phrasing (e.g., *Reserva Exclusiva*).
- **Body & Operational Roles (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean geometry, open counters, and high legibility at micro-sizes for technical specifications (m², suites, coordinates, VR prompts).
- **Uppercase Badges & Metadata (`label-caps`):** Rendered with an expansive letter-spacing (+0.12em) in warm gold or muted platinum to evoke luxury horology dials and architectural title blocks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large-format real estate photography without visual clutter.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (320px - 767px):` 4-column layout, `gutter-mobile` (16px), `margin-mobile` (20px). Bottom navigation and drawer sheets anchor interactive controls.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1023px):` 8-column layout, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Two-column listing cards.
  - `Desktop (1024px - 1440px+):` 12-column layout, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (48px+), max-width bounded at 1440px to retain intimate editorial composure.

- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - Component internals strictly utilize `space-xs` (6px) through `space-md` (20px).
  - Section dividers leverage `space-xl` (56px) to maintain a serene, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, deep-toned diffusion, hairline edge highlights, and satin glassmorphism rather than muddy drop-shadows.

- **Surface Levels:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#121316`, completely light-absorbent.
  - **Level 1 (Card & Section):** Solid `#1A1C20` or translucent `rgba(26, 28, 32, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop blur and an inner hairline stroke: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
  - **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Bottom Sheets):** `#22252A` with an ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
  - **Level 3 (Interactive Spatial HUD / 360 Modals):** Ultra-thin glass `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, creating an instrument-like heads-up display.

- **Glow & Sheen:**
  - Key focus rings and spatial triggers utilize a soft gold illumination: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a refined curvature scale anchored on `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 0.5rem/8px, reaching up to 1rem/16px for cards and 1.5rem/24px for `rounded-2xl` sheet elements):

- **Cards & Media Viewports:** `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) creates an architectural framing effect that softens raw photography.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** `rounded-xl` (12px) balances contemporary tactility with structured dignity.
- **Pill Elements (Status Badges, Spatial Chips):** Fully rounded pill caps (`9999px`) provide contrast against rectangular cards.
- **Floating Action Controls:** Spherical circles (56px diameter) for the concierge WhatsApp trigger and 360° gyro toggles.

## Components

### Property Cards
- **Architecture:** Contained within `#1A1C20` backing with `rounded-2xl` corners and 1px muted metallic border.
- **Image Showcase:** 16:10 or 4:3 aspect ratio, subtle scale-up (+3%) on hover over 600ms cubic-bezier transition.
- **Badges:** Absolute positioned top-left for availability (`EXCLUSIVIDADE`, `LANÇAMENTO`) in gold pill, top-right for `360º TOUR VIRTUAL` pill combining violet glow with a compass icon.
- **Metadata Stack:** Typographic footer displaying architectural attributes (m², bedrooms, suites, private garage slots) partitioned by delicate vertical rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Luxe Button:** Subtle linear gradient from `#E5C365` to `#C29B27`, dark charcoal text (`#121316`) in bold weight, satin bevel highlight on top border. Hover state increases inner luminosity.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Charcoal surface with gold hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`), gold text, transitioning to solid gold wash on interaction.
- **Concierge Trigger:** Floating action button (FAB) at bottom-right, deep emerald glass background, metallic WhatsApp icon, accompanied by a discreet pulsing status ring.

### Spatial 360° HUD & WebXR Controls
- **Viewer Overlay:** Floating glass bar at the bottom center of the panoramic canvas.
- **Controls:** Micro-buttons for Gyroscope toggle, Fullscreen, Floorplan hotspot overlay, and VR Headset (WebXR) mode.
- **Hotspots:** Concentric pulsing rings in champagne gold pointing to rooms, finishes, and panoramic outdoor views of Santo Antônio de Jesus.

### Filter Bottom Sheets (Mobile & Tablet)
- **Container:** Slides from bottom with `rounded-t-3xl`, backdropped by dark blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Handle:** Champagne satin pill bar at top center.
- **Controls:** Dual-handle price and square-footage sliders in `#D4AF37`, accompanied by toggle chips with soft tactile gold fills when active.

### Inputs & Selection
- **Field Fields:** Recessed charcoal fill (`#15171A`), 1px neutral stroke shifting to `#D4AF37` upon focus. Placeholder typography in refined off-white muted to 40% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom geometric selectors with gold checkmarks and smooth ease-in transformations.
